# Language Study Planner

A study system for busy self-directed language learners who want to turn resources they already own into consistent, measurable practice.

## Language

**Resource**:
A learner-owned item used for language study, such as an article, video, podcast episode, textbook section, or saved lesson.
_Avoid_: content, lesson

**Study Plan**:
A realistic, time-bounded sequence of practice activities selected from a learner's Resources.
_Avoid_: schedule, curriculum

**Practice Session**:
One completed block of language study from a Study Plan.
_Avoid_: lesson, task

**Target Language**:
The language a learner has chosen to practise. French is the only Target Language supported in the first version; the product should be able to add others later.
_Avoid_: course language, language track

**Busy Learner**:
A person who wants to learn a language but must fit short, intentional Practice Sessions around competing work and life commitments.
_Avoid_: casual learner, user

**Learning Evidence**:
A completed piece of work that demonstrates a learner's developing ability, such as a finished passage, saved writing, or Speaking Check recording.
_Avoid_: points, streak, XP

**Capability**:
An explicit, plain-language statement of what a learner can do in a real situation, earned through a completed Capability Check. A Capability does not claim general language proficiency.
_Avoid_: level, score, mastery

**Capability Check**:
Three related challenges that produce Learning Evidence for a Capability: a supported attempt, a variation, and a delayed Review Mission. The challenges test use in changing situations, not memorisation of one answer.
_Avoid_: test, final exam, proficiency assessment

**Mission**:
A short, scenario-based Practice Session that ends by earning a Capability and creating Learning Evidence.
_Avoid_: lesson, drill, level

**Difficulty Ramp**:
The deliberate progression within a Mission from supported repetition to varied, multi-sentence production, keeping practice both solvable and challenging.
_Avoid_: random difficulty, grinding

**Word Card**:
A compact, in-context explanation of a useful French word or reusable sentence pattern, including its meaning, example, and pronunciation support.
_Avoid_: vocabulary list, flashcard

**Review**:
A short reappearance of a Word Card or sentence pattern in a later Mission so that the learner recalls it in a new context.
_Avoid_: cramming, drilling

**Speaking Check**:
A practice activity in which a learner speaks a target phrase, records and replays their attempt, and may see a browser-generated transcript when the browser supports it.
_Avoid_: pronunciation grade, speech assessment
